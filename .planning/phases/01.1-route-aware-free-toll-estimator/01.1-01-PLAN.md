<objective>
Replace the crude flat speed-fraction heuristic inside `TollService` with a **route-aware free estimate**: sum the autoroute kilometres actually on the route (from Mapbox `steps[].ref`) minus a free-autoroute exclusion list, and multiply by a sourced national-average €/km. This is the whole vertical slice of Phase 1.1 — Mapbox steps data path → `TripsService` threading → route-aware classification + rate in `TollService` → a better `tollCost` out of `POST /trips/calculate`, still labelled "≈ estimé".

Purpose: Deliver TOLL-07 (route-aware estimate-primary toll, fully free) and TOLL-02 refined (no-steps callers still get the heuristic; user still sees an estimate) per locked decisions LD-1..LD-7. No paid API, no key, no card, no schema change, no UI change.
Output: route-aware `estimateFrenchTolls` + classifier/constants in `backend/src/toll/`, Mapbox `steps=true` surfaced on `DirectionsResult`, call site threaded, unit spec extended, trips e2e kept green.
</objective>
