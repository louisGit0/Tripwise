<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <what-built>
    The trip result page (`/app/trips/result`) rebuilt in the editorial-premium-dark system: serif
    route title, animated mono hero cost counter, segmented Énergie/Péage breakdown bar, token-driven
    multi-energy comparison bars, designed interaction states, and a layout-mirroring loading skeleton.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Calculate a trip on `/app/dashboard` (Address mode) that uses tolled autoroutes (e.g. Paris → Lyon)
       so a non-zero toll appears, then land on `/app/trips/result`.
    2. Hero: confirm the total cost figure ANIMATES upward smoothly on load (no jank), renders in
       JetBrains Mono with fixed 2 decimals, and the digits do NOT shift/reflow while counting (no layout shift).
    3. Title: confirm the route title (`Origin → Destination`) is in the editorial serif (Instrument Serif),
       not the previous sans heading.
    4. Breakdown bar: confirm a single segmented bar shows Énergie (energy color) + Péage (warm taupe, not amber),
       with a legend (`Énergie · …` / `Péage · …`) and the réel/≈ estimé pill+tooltip on the Péage value.
    5. Calculate a toll-free trip (or a short urban one): confirm the Péage segment AND its legend DISAPPEAR
       and a single full-width energy bar shows instead.
    6. Comparison: confirm the gas/diesel/EV/(gpl) bars are cheapest-first, the current energy is highlighted
       (full color + `← actuel` accent marker), others are muted — and the colors match the energy palette
       (NOT emerald/sky/violet/amber).
    7. Interaction: tab through the page — every interactive element shows a blue accent focus ring; the passenger
       stepper +/- have hover/press states; reduce passengers/increase and confirm the hero re-counts to the new per-person total.
    8. Reduced motion: enable OS "reduce motion" (or DevTools → Rendering → Emulate prefers-reduced-motion: reduce),
       reload — confirm the hero shows the final value instantly (no count-up), bars appear at full width with no reveal,
       and the skeleton (briefly visible on load) does not pulse.
    9. Theme: toggle light mode in settings — confirm the page still reads correctly (surfaces/toll/gpl tokens have light values).
  </how-to-verify>
